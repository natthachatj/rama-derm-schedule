# Approval inbox, case cards and appearance

The main Overview now combines pending clinical submissions, leave requests and presentations in Your first priority. Category filters show their counts; Time-sensitive first and Newest submissions first are both available. Initially five items appear, with View all pending for the remainder. Review opens the exact case, leave request or presentation in its existing workflow. Clinical submissions span cohorts; leave and presentations use the selected academic year. The Clinical Overview starts with its own owner-only pending-submission inbox. Approval removes the same entry from both views. Drafts, historical imports, approved and trashed clinical records are excluded.

The home inbox reads a narrow owner-only index projection: registry type, case UUID, fellow/cohort and submission time. It includes no patient identifiers, answers, folder URLs or clinical summary. Nonowners are denied before accessing Google-backed clinical storage. Loading and connection failures are shown separately from an empty inbox. Visible pages refresh every three minutes and after clinical workflow changes. Existing approval authorization and validation remain authoritative.

Registry cards have separate borders, spacing and tinted headers. Owner cards prominently show the actual First visit date in bold; submission/update dates remain secondary. Fellows retain receipt-only access. Reminder red dots, named missing-field navigation and quiet reminder preferences remain available.

Preference is stored against the authenticated account; each response exposes only that user's preference. Public visitors cannot change account settings. Dark styling covers scheduling, forms, clinical registry, dashboards and chart labels. Signature canvases retain white paper. Phone header controls stay on one row with accessible icon labels.
