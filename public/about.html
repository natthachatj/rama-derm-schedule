RAMA DERM Sx Fellowship Workspace

One workspace for fellowship schedules, teaching activities, leave coverage and structured clinical case submissions.

View published scheduleSign in to your workspace

Schedules and coverage

View published clinic assignments by academic year. Authorized fellows and administrators manage leave requests, replacement coverage and their permitted workspace information.

Teaching and presentations

Organize lectures, topic reviews, journal clubs, workshops and meetings. Track presentation submissions and approvals according to your account permissions.

Private clinical registry

Authorized fellows submit RAVC complication cases or injectable-related nodule cases using structured forms. They can edit their own unfinished drafts. After submission, they receive a reference and confirmation; complete submitted records are restricted to the designated clinical owner.

Google Sheets and Drive

The clinical owner separately authorizes Google access to save registry information in the configured spreadsheets and create new-case folders and information-only PDF summaries in the configured Drive location. PDF and editable PowerPoint exports contain submitted information. The owner manages photos and videos manually in Drive.

The published schedule is publicly viewable. Patient information, private clinical records and Google connection credentials are not part of the public schedule.

Your account and data

Google sign-in identifies your account. Staff-ID/password access may be available to authorized fellows. Clinical-owner access requires the designated Google identity and is separate from schedule-administrator access.

Read the privacy policy for information about collection, permissions, storage, retention and requests to the workspace administrator. For help, use your programme's administrator contact or the support email displayed on this app's Google consent screen.
