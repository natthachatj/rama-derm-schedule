RAMA DERM Sx Fellowship Workspace

Privacy policy

Last updated: 7 November 2026

This policy describes how this fellowship workspace accesses, uses, stores and shares information for scheduling, teaching administration and its private clinical registry. It applies to the application; Google and Netlify also operate their services under their own policies.

1. Information collected

  • Account information: Google account identifier, verified email and name for Google sign-in; or staff-ID/username and protected password-verification data for supported password accounts.
  • Fellowship information: cohort and fellow details, clinic assignments, leave requests and handover notes, coverage, teaching activities, presentation records and approvals.
  • Clinical submissions: information entered in case forms, including patient identifiers where supplied, referral details, injection history, clinical findings, investigations, treatments, follow-ups and outcomes. Drafts, submitted records, original imported answers, case references and revision history may be retained.
  • Technical information: authentication/session information, request and security information used for hosting and access protection, integration status and processing errors. Hosting providers may process IP addresses and operational logs.

2. How information is used

Information is used to authenticate accounts, enforce permissions, publish approved schedules, manage leave and teaching workflows, save drafts and submitted cases, support owner review and corrections, produce private analyses and information-only exports, maintain audit records and troubleshoot processing. The registry is not an advertising service and does not send clinical information to an AI service.

3. Google user data and permissions

Ordinary Google sign-in requests identity information. A separate clinical-owner connection requests Google Drive and Google Sheets permissions for offline access. Those requested Google permissions are broad; they are not a Google-enforced restriction to one folder or two spreadsheets. Application code uses the configured registry spreadsheets and case-folder destination for its registry functions.

The app reads original spreadsheet responses for reviewed historical import and writes app records, source copies, settings and revisions to the configured spreadsheets. For new submitted cases it creates a case folder and information-only PDF summary in the configured Drive destination. It checks the sharing of the configured resources. Historical imports do not automatically create folders or move existing files.

Photos and videos are uploaded and managed manually by the owner in Google Drive. The registry does not upload, browse or embed that media in its exports. Existing file links may be preserved as record information.

Use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements. Google user data is used to provide the described user-facing functions; it is not sold or used for advertising or general-purpose AI model training.

4. Who can access information

Public visitors can view published scheduling and other explicitly public workspace content. They cannot browse the clinical registry. Authorized fellows can access their permitted scheduling and teaching information, manage their own unfinished clinical drafts and submit cases. After submission, fellow access is limited to a confirmation/reference rather than the complete clinical record.

The designated clinical owner can review submitted clinical records, make later changes, inspect history, analyze and export them. Being a scheduling administrator alone does not grant clinical-owner access. Google and Netlify process information as infrastructure providers for the configured functions. If optional email-code login is enabled, the configured email delivery provider processes the delivery address and authentication message.

Sharing settings and any copies or exports made outside the app must also be managed by the owner. Information may be disclosed where legally required.

5. Storage and safeguards

Clinical records and saved revisions are stored in the configured Google Sheets. Case folders and automatic PDFs are stored in the configured Google Drive. Netlify provides hosting and server functions, and its private storage supports scheduling state, account records, encrypted owner Google credentials, opaque processing locks and isolated fictional preview data.

The app uses HTTPS, authenticated backend checks and role restrictions. Owner Google refresh credentials are encrypted server-side and are not sent to fellows or included in frontend code. Patient records and Google credentials are not stored in the GitHub source repository. These safeguards do not eliminate every security risk.

6. Retention, corrections and deletion

Submitted cases, source copies and revision history remain until the clinical owner arranges appropriate retention or deletion. Unsubmitted drafts inactive for more than 90 days are flagged for owner review; they are not automatically deleted. Deleting an active draft does not automatically erase its saved revision history. Drive files, exports, historical documents and provider backups may need separate management.

Fellows can correct their own unfinished drafts. After submission, contact the clinical owner for corrections or additional follow-up. For access, correction, retention or deletion requests, contact the workspace administrator through your fellowship programme or the support email shown on this app's Google consent screen. Requests must be checked against authorization and applicable clinical-record retention obligations.

7. Sessions and revoking Google access

Necessary session cookies support sign-in and access protection. Signing out ends the app session; it does not delete records or disconnect the owner's background Google authorization. You can revoke the app's Google connection through your Google Account connections. Revocation stops future authorized API access, but does not automatically erase information or files already saved.

8. Exports and updates to this policy

Presentation exports default to anonymous information-only summaries. The owner must review free text and the export preview; automatic omission of known identifiers is not a guarantee of anonymity. Private exports remain sensitive and should be handled accordingly.

Changes to application functionality or data practices may require an updated policy. The date above identifies this version. Contact the workspace administrator if you have questions about these practices.
